 Key MeaningsThoroughness and Care: The quality of being detailed, careful, and exact (e.g., scientific rigor or intellectual rigor).Strictness: Harsh inflexibility or severe adherence to rules and standards (e.g., the rigor of the law).Hardship (usually plural rigors): Severe, difficult, or unpleasant conditions (e.g., the rigors of winter or rigors of military training).Stiffness: Medical or physical rigidity, such as rigor mortis (stiffness after death).
